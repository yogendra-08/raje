# **App Name**: RajyaBazaar

## Core Features:

- Homepage Hero Banner: Display a hero banner featuring a quote from Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj alongside an image of Raigad Fort.
- Product Categories Display: Showcase product categories such as Handicrafts, Royal Attire, Historical Books, Wooden Weapons, and Souvenirs in a visually appealing layout.
- Trending Items Showcase: Display trending items like limited edition swords, turbans, shields, and paintings to highlight popular products.
- Themed Product Cards: Implement royal product cards with cloth/wood textures, including a "Maharaj’s Pick" badge for selected items.
- Themed Cart and Checkout: Design the cart and checkout page with a thematic shield-style progress bar and an animated sword icon for order confirmation.
- User Authentication: Implement a user authentication system with a "Rajyabhishek" login greeting and role-based access (User/Seller/Admin).
- AI Powered Product Setup: AI powered Styling tool suggesting stylings with Maratha empire references and the ability to purchase all relevant items for your product setup.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Deep red (#8B0000) to represent royalty and valor. This deep hue provides a sense of richness and heritage.
- Background color: Light beige (#F5F5DC) with a desaturated tone to mimic the look of aged parchment, creating a historic and rustic feel.
- Accent color: Saffron (#FF9933), analogous to deep red, evoking Maratha pride and cultural richness, while also creating contrast for key elements.
- Headline font: 'Playfair', a modern sans-serif with thin-thick lines that evoke elegance and high-end style.
- Body font: 'PT Sans' for body text, pairing with 'Playfair' to offer a clean, readable experience for longer content.
- Use icons inspired by Maratha heritage such as swords, shields, forts, tigers, and turbans, crafted with a rustic, hand-drawn style.
- Incorporate subtle animations like fluttering Maratha flags and product hover shines to enhance the user experience without being intrusive.
